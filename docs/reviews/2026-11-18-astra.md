# SignForge 2 review · 18 November 2026

At `origin/master` (`5c08bb8be6ec`), the most urgent fix is to bind the device bearer credential to its intended API. The supervisor checks who asks for the secret, but the player lets its launch URL choose who receives it. The backend has stronger boundaries: membership is checked per request, the dispatcher intersects grants with roles, and database mutations and audit records commit together. The previous review led to substantive fixes in template containment, streaming downloads, drafts and browser coverage. The remaining reliability problems show up when two events coincide, especially a clock reset during offline playback, a retry after re-imaging, or a publish during another edit. Master also fails its required typecheck.
